> Our goal was to bring teaching closer to current requirements for education and at the same time take into account current trends in business based on the idea of ​​networking, sharing and co-creation. This project reflected the needs of LS clients (to change the form and content of teaching so that the student becomes an active participant in the course and at the same time can focus not only on language, but also personal development) as well as the needs of language school and their lecturers (responding to teaching trends, adapting teaching to clients and using conversation and game-learning techniques).
>

> 
> The main goal of the project was to create a sustainable business model for language schools. This required: (1) to innovate the existing business model of language schools, (2) to create new products that will better reflect the current requirements of language school clients, (3) to create instructions for trainers so that they become leaders of the teaching process, (4) create tutorials for students to learn more easily, (5) create an online platform that connects course providers with their clients.

These three activities also had a connection to the collaboration of new products (activity 4). Activity 5 was based on brainstorming, with the help of which the limits of the current business model were identified and the features of the new business model were proposed in relation to the findings that resulted from activity 1-4. Activities 6 and 7 were implemented as pilot trainings for trainers and clients of language schools. Activity 8 led to the creation of an online Webinar, the content of which consists of partial activities of the project and its outputs.
